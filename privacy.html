Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Plausibly Impossible runs a private publishing tool that uploads the channel's own original videos to the channel's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts. It is not offered to the public.

What we access

When the channel owner signs in, the tool receives only the permissions it needs: uploading and managing the channel's own videos, reading their view and engagement statistics, and sending email from the channel's own Gmail address to itself (to deliver post captions). It does not read anyone's email.

How we use it

Only to publish the channel's videos, measure how they perform, and notify the channel owner. We never sell, rent or share this data.

Storage

Sign-in tokens and statistics are stored only on the channel owner's computer.

Other people's data

We do not collect personal data from viewers or any other users.

Google user data

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google), in TikTok under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions, and in Facebook/Instagram under Settings → Business integrations.
